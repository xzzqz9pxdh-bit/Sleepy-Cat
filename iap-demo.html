Steps

Launch the app and allow photo library access. The photo grid appears.

  1. Collage — com.finalray.pro.puzzle
    From the photo grid, select two or more photos, then tap Collage. In the Templates list, select one of the premium templates (those listed after the free templates). The export button in the top-right turns red. Tap it to open the purchase sheet, then tap the price button to start the purchase.
  2. Filters — com.finalray.pro.filter
    Return to the photo grid and tap any single photo to open the editor. Tap Adjust in the bottom toolbar. The first card in the list is Filters, with a lock icon next to its title. Tap the title or the lock icon to open the purchase sheet.
  3. Color mix — com.finalray.pro.hsl
    In the editor, tap Adjust and scroll the card list down to the Color mix card. Tap its title or the lock icon next to it.
  4. Curve — com.finalray.pro.curve
    In the editor, tap Adjust and scroll further down to the Curve card. Tap its title or the lock icon next to it.
  5. Masking — com.finalray.pro.masking
    In the editor, tap Mask in the bottom toolbar and add a mask (for example, Brush). Once a mask layer exists, the mask layer panel header shows a lock icon. Tap the panel title or the lock icon to open the purchase sheet.

Two further locations (not in the recording)

A combined purchase screen: apply a filter, a Color mix adjustment, or a Curve adjustment to a photo, then tap Export followed by Next. A summary sheet lists every paid feature currently in use, each with its own price button that starts the purchase.

Restore Purchases: from the photo grid, tap the gear icon at the top-left of the screen to open Settings, then scroll to the OTHER section. The first row is Restore Purchases.
