This page lists every location in the app from which an In-App Purchase can be started, and describes what was changed in build 1.0 (3) in response to the previous review.

Important: the test account may already own some of these items

During the previous review, the purchases were completed successfully at the App Store level; the defect was that our app failed to record them. Because all five items are non-consumable, those purchases remain permanently associated with the sandbox Apple Account that made them.

If this build is reviewed with the same sandbox Apple Account, the affected features will now correctly appear already unlocked, and no purchase sheet will be presented for them. This is the fix working as intended, and not a missing or non-functional In-App Purchase.

To exercise the purchase flow, please use a new sandbox Apple Account, or test one of the five items that was not purchased during the previous review. Tapping Restore Purchases will also report which items the current account already owns.

What was fixed in build 1.0 (3)

The previous build did not reconcile completed purchases with StoreKit, which is why a purchase could complete without unlocking its feature, and why the purchase flow could be presented again from the beginning.

  • Entitlements are now reconciled with Transaction.currentEntitlements at launch, when the app returns to the foreground, and immediately before any purchase sheet is presented. The previous build relied only on local state written at the moment of purchase, so a purchase that completed without the app recording it was never recovered.
  • A Transaction.updates listener is installed at launch and kept for the lifetime of the app, so transactions that arrive outside a purchase call (Ask to Buy approvals, Family Sharing changes, purchases made on another device) are applied instead of being dropped.
  • All transactions are now finished, including any left unfinished by the previous build. Unfinished transactions were one reason the same purchase flow could reappear.
  • Deferred (pending) and failed purchases are now reported to the customer explicitly. In the previous build both cases produced no visible feedback, which made a pending purchase indistinguishable from a failed one and invited a repeated purchase attempt.
  • A Restore Purchases button was added to the top-left corner of every purchase sheet, in addition to the existing one in Settings.

The five In-App Purchases

All five are non-consumable one-time unlocks. They are not restricted by storefront, region, or account configuration, and are available on all storefronts. Each one is surfaced from the tool it unlocks, rather than from a single store screen.

Steps

Launch the app and allow photo library access. The photo grid appears.

  1. Collage — com.finalray.pro.puzzle
    From the photo grid, select two or more photos, then tap Collage. In the Templates list, select one of the premium templates (those listed after the free templates). The export button in the top-right turns red. Tap it to open the purchase sheet, then tap the price button to start the purchase.
  2. Filters — com.finalray.pro.filter
    Return to the photo grid and tap any single photo to open the editor. Tap Adjust in the bottom toolbar. The first card in the list is Filters, with a lock icon next to its title. Tap the title or the lock icon to open the purchase sheet.
  3. Color mix — com.finalray.pro.hsl
    In the editor, tap Adjust and scroll the card list down to the Color mix card. Tap its title or the lock icon next to it.
  4. Curve — com.finalray.pro.curve
    In the editor, tap Adjust and scroll further down to the Curve card. Tap its title or the lock icon next to it.
  5. Masking — com.finalray.pro.masking
    In the editor, tap Mask in the bottom toolbar and add a mask (for example, Brush). Once a mask layer exists, the mask layer panel header shows a lock icon. Tap the panel title or the lock icon to open the purchase sheet.

Two further locations (not in the recording)

A combined purchase screen: apply a filter, a Color mix adjustment, or a Curve adjustment to a photo, then tap Export followed by Next. A summary sheet titled “Unlock the following features to export” lists every paid feature currently in use, each with its own price button that starts the purchase.

Restore Purchases: from the photo grid, tap the gear icon at the top-left of the screen to open Settings, then scroll to the OTHER section. The first row is Restore Purchases. The same action is also available from the top-left corner of every purchase sheet.
