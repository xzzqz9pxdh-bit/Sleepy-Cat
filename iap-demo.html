This page shows every location in the app from which an In-App Purchase can be started.

Before you begin: the test account may already own some of these items

All five items are non-consumable one-time unlocks. If the Apple Account used for testing has already purchased one of them, that feature will appear already unlocked and no purchase sheet will be presented for it.

To exercise the purchase flow, please use a new sandbox Apple Account, or choose an item that has not been purchased before on the account in use. Tapping Restore Purchases reports which items the current account already owns.

The five In-App Purchases

All five are non-consumable one-time unlocks, available on all storefronts with no regional or account restrictions. Each one is offered from the tool it unlocks, rather than from a single store screen.

  • Collage — com.finalray.pro.puzzle
  • Filters — com.finalray.pro.filter
  • Color mix — com.finalray.pro.hsl
  • Curve — com.finalray.pro.curve
  • Masking — com.finalray.pro.masking

Steps

Launch the app and allow photo library access. The photo grid appears.

  1. Collage — com.finalray.pro.puzzle
    From the photo grid, select two or more photos, then tap Collage. In the Templates list, select one of the premium templates (those listed after the free templates). The export button in the top-right turns red. Tap it to open the purchase sheet, then tap the price button to start the purchase.
  2. Filters — com.finalray.pro.filter
    Return to the photo grid and tap any single photo to open the editor. Tap Adjust in the bottom toolbar. The first card in the list is Filters, with a lock icon next to its title. Tap the title or the lock icon to open the purchase sheet.
  3. Color mix — com.finalray.pro.hsl
    In the editor, tap Adjust and scroll the card list down to the Color mix card. Tap its title or the lock icon next to it.
  4. Curve — com.finalray.pro.curve
    In the editor, tap Adjust and scroll further down to the Curve card. Tap its title or the lock icon next to it.
  5. Masking — com.finalray.pro.masking
    In the editor, tap Mask in the bottom toolbar. The mask card at the bottom of the screen has a lock button at its right end, immediately to the right of the add (+) button. Tap it to open the purchase sheet.

Two further locations (not in the recording)

A combined purchase screen: apply a filter, a Color mix adjustment, or a Curve adjustment to a photo, then tap Export followed by Next. A summary sheet titled “Unlock the following features to export” lists every paid feature currently in use, each with its own price button that starts the purchase.

Restore Purchases: from the photo grid, tap the gear icon at the top-left of the screen to open Settings, then scroll to the OTHER section. The first row is Restore Purchases. The same action is also available from the top-left corner of every purchase sheet.
